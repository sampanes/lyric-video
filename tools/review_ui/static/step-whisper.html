Step 2 — Whisper Timing

What This Step Does
Run WhisperX on the audio to produce a rough first-pass timing. WhisperX's word labels may be wrong on cute / mumbled vocals, but its timestamps are usually accurate. The mapper grafts those timestamps onto your written lyrics, then renders a throwaway placeholder MP4. You only care about timing.json — Step 3 lets you fine-tune it.
What This Will Run
python scripts\make_videos.py "your-slug" --refresh-whisper
